About

We are four lifelong friends who have shared a deep bond for decades.

United by our love for exceptional coffee and a shared dream, we founded Intika Coffeehouse — a place where friendship, passion, and connection come together in every cup.

At Intika Coffeehouse, we believe coffee is connection — a universal language that brings people together across cultures and continents. From the highlands of Ethiopia to the mountains of Bolivia passing through Brazil and Colombia, every origin tells its own story. We honour these stories by sourcing high-quality green beans and roasting them fresh every day on Ireland’s first Air-Motion Coffee Roaster.

Our open-concept roastery in Dublin is designed as a place where transparency meets flavour. Here you can see the entire bean-to-cup journey, enjoy expertly brewed coffee on our Dalla Corte machines, and experience speciality drinks that celebrate the diversity and richness of coffee from around the world.

Whether you join us for a morning Flat White, a rich Cappuccino, or a carefully brewed V60 filter, our goal is simple: to create moments of connection through every cup.

Welcome to Intika — where great coffee brings us all closer.

The Heart of Our Roastery

As the official representatives for Air-Motion Coffee Roasters in Ireland, we proudly operate the country’s first Air-Motion roaster. This innovative technology delivers cleaner, sweeter, and more consistent roasts every day — bringing exceptional flavour and transparency from green bean to cup.

Dalla Corte — The Art of Espresso

As the official representatives for Dalla Corte in Ireland, we bring world-class Italian engineering to every cup. These machines deliver unmatched temperature stability, perfect extraction, and beautiful flavour in every shot.

From the advanced Zero Plus Barista with flow control to professional reliability — this is the heart of our espresso bar at Intika Coffeehouse.

Bring Dalla Corte to Your Café – Contact Us

Our Green Bean Trading Centre

We import high-quality specialty green coffee from trusted origins in Bolivia, Brazil, Ethiopia, Colombia, and beyond.

At Intika’s open trading centre, we welcome coffee importers, roasters, and producers to bring their beans, trade, cup, and roast together.

Because coffee is connection — a shared journey from farm to cup.

Because coffee has the power to bring people together — from the farmers who grow it, to the hands that roast it, to the moments we share over every cup.

At Intika, every bean tells a story of connection: between cultures, between people, and between you and a perfect moment.
